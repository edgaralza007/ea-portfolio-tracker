About your signals

News covers the last 30 days. Calendar dates use UTC; quote and refresh times use your local time. Estimates are labeled. Conference dates appear only when a headline includes a specific date; undated announcements stay below the calendar.

Opening the app checks for updates. Refresh rechecks sources, with a 30-second minimum between requests per ticker. Normal cache: 5 minutes. Quotes can be delayed. This watchlist does not calculate portfolio value.

Holdings and the last retrieved data are saved in this browser. Free public feeds may be incomplete or unavailable. No API key is needed.
